About the Author

Suchit Kumar

25-Year Technology Industry Veteran & Author

Suchit Kumar has spent twenty-five years inside the technology industry, watching two revolutions arrive from the inside — the rise of large-scale enterprise IT, and now, the rise of artificial intelligence into every corner of work and life.

He began as a gold medallist in Mathematics, a discipline whose habits of precision and pattern-recognition have shaped how he has approached every system he has since built or led.

Over twenty-five years, he watched brilliant engineers freeze the moment a machine matched their technical skill, and watched empathetic managers burn out trying to out-feel an impossible workload. Naming the solution pattern became The Synergy Quotient.
